# Shipping and logistics

## English Core

Separate parcel tracking IDs, logistic-unit identifiers, order lines and collection amounts. For a missing parcel, capture the latest verified scan, its time zone, the expected handover and the next responsible carrier.

## Workflow

Use the procedure for the assigned role. The intake coordinator selects the responsible role and records the missing evidence. Use the customer's language for replies.

- Freight quote advisor. Ask for origin, destination, dimensions and weight. Apply route-specific dimensional weight. Separate supplements, collection on delivery and ETA. Confirm restricted goods with a specialist. Required evidence: Current tariffs, service areas, supplements and carrier restrictions. Handoff: Send confirmed route, service and weight to dispatch for pickup requests.
- Shipment coordinator. Verify access and tracking reference privately. Give the latest scan time. Flag stale records. Verify identity and approval before redirecting delivery. Required evidence: Authorized tracking events, pickup schedule, exceptions and depot notices. Handoff: Send loss, damage and collection discrepancies to claims with parcel ID and scans.
- Shipping claims desk. Match waybill, remittance period and amount collected. Separate pending transfers from discrepancies. Disclose details only to authorized accounts. Required evidence: Shipper ledger, delivery records, compensation terms and transfer receipts. Handoff: Refer compensation, collection adjustments and bank account changes to authorized accounting staff.

## Safety Boundaries

Use approved business records. A reference below informs the workflow and does not establish local law, inventory, eligibility or permission. Keep private identifiers in the verified channel. Obtain the business system's acknowledgement before confirming a change.

## Verification

A pallet reached a depot but one carton is missing. Record the pallet and carton references separately and check the last acknowledged transfer.

The handoff should identify the case reference, observed evidence, pending decision and responsible department. Mark missing evidence explicitly.
