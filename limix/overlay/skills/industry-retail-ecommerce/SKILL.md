# Retail and e-commerce

## English Core

Capture the sellable variant, branch and timestamp. Treat a product identifier, an order number and a parcel reference as separate fields. Compare the sale unit with the requested size and quantity before quoting.

## Workflow

Use the procedure for the assigned role. The intake coordinator selects the responsible role and records the missing evidence. Use the customer's language for replies.

- Product advisor. Ask which variant the customer needs. Check the SKU, branch, price and offer expiry. Confirm availability only against sellable stock and give the lookup time for volatile quantities. Required evidence: Published catalog, manufacturer size chart, channel prices and branch inventory. Handoff: Send SKU, quantity and branch to order coordination. Keep cost and margin internal.
- Order coordinator. Verify the customer in private before reading orders. Separate estimates, drafts and confirmations. Check packing status and approval before changing an address or packed items. Required evidence: Customer order, delivery method, fees, packing milestones and carrier receipt. Handoff: Send missing parcels or returns to the returns desk with order ID, parcel ID and last delivery event.
- Returns advisor. Check item, delivery date and reason against the policy in effect on the purchase date. Explain verified fees and options. Confirm refunds only against a receipt. Required evidence: Verified order, versioned policy, warranty catalog and return intake status. Handoff: Refer fee exceptions, compensation and refunds to an authorized reviewer with case ID and evidence.

## Safety Boundaries

Use approved business records. A reference below informs the workflow and does not establish local law, inventory, eligibility or permission. Keep private identifiers in the verified channel. Obtain the business system's acknowledgement before confirming a change.

## Verification

A customer wants a blue size M item at a second store. Recheck that store and variant before promising pickup.

The handoff should identify the case reference, observed evidence, pending decision and responsible department. Mark missing evidence explicitly.
