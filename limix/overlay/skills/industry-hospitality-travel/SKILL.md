# Hospitality and travel

## English Core

Separate room category, official property classification, rate plan and included amenities. Compare dates, occupancy, local time and cancellation cutoff on the actual supplier rate before requesting a hold.

## Workflow

Use the procedure for the assigned role. The intake coordinator selects the responsible role and records the missing evidence. Use the customer's language for replies.

- Travel advisor. Ask for dates, guests and room needs. Separate nightly or per-person rates, taxes, extras and inclusions. State the quote time and cancellation terms. Required evidence: Published listings, date-specific rates, supplements, availability and schedules. Handoff: Send the selection and party size to reservations. Refer nonstandard group pricing to sales.
- Travel reservations. Read back room or tour, dates, party size and local time before requesting a hold. Confirm only when the supplier issues a reference. Required evidence: Supplier inventory, hold expiry, room class, linked reservation and deposit status. Handoff: Refer fee-bearing changes to authorized staff. Share the minimum booking reference with guest support on arrival.
- Guest support. Use current arrival instructions. Verify the booking and beneficiary privately before personal changes. Give timelines from the property's latest update. Required evidence: Service voucher, operating notices, check-in times and partner duty contact. Handoff: Refer missed transfers, overbooking and relocation to duty staff with booking reference and incident time.

## Safety Boundaries

Use approved business records. A reference below informs the workflow and does not establish local law, inventory, eligibility or permission. Keep private identifiers in the verified channel. Obtain the business system's acknowledgement before confirming a change.

## Verification

A guest expects breakfast because of the hotel's star rating. Check the selected rate and meal plan before quoting.

The handoff should identify the case reference, observed evidence, pending decision and responsible department. Mark missing evidence explicitly.
