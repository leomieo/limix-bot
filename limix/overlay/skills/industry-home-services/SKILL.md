# Home services

## English Core

Distinguish a remote estimate from an on-site assessment. Capture the equipment and observed symptoms, service area, access restrictions, visit fee, technician skill and acceptance criteria. Escalate signs of a hazard to a qualified technician.

## Workflow

Use the procedure for the assigned role. The intake coordinator selects the responsible role and records the missing evidence. Use the customer's language for replies.

- Home service intake. Ask about equipment, area and symptoms. Explain survey fees, inclusions and charges requiring inspection. Escalate hazards to qualified staff. Required evidence: Service catalog, coverage area, published fees and urgent referral criteria. Handoff: Send job type and area to dispatch. Collect the exact address privately when booking.
- Field dispatcher. Check area, skills and time slot. Share the address only with assigned staff. Promise arrival only after confirmation and update the customer if delayed. Required evidence: Approved technician schedule, travel zones, shift confirmation and authorized contact details. Handoff: Send the job reference and accepted work items to warranty support after completion.
- Home service warranty desk. Check the customer job, scope, completion date and warranty period. Ask which symptoms persist and prepare a scoped reinspection request. Required evidence: Acceptance record, warranty policy, fee receipt and available revisit slots. Handoff: Refer disputed charges and property damage to the area manager with job ID. Give the customer a tracking reference.

## Safety Boundaries

Use approved business records. A reference below informs the workflow and does not establish local law, inventory, eligibility or permission. Keep private identifiers in the verified channel. Obtain the business system's acknowledgement before confirming a change.

## Verification

A customer asks for guaranteed savings from insulation. Record the building conditions and arrange an assessment before making a property-specific estimate.

The handoff should identify the case reference, observed evidence, pending decision and responsible department. Mark missing evidence explicitly.
