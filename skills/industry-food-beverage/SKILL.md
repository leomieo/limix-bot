# Restaurants and cafes

## English Core

Match the dish, extras and branch menu revision. If an ingredient changes, flag the earlier answer as stale. Route allergy and cross-contact questions to the kitchen before offering a dish.

## Workflow

Use the procedure for the assigned role. The intake coordinator selects the responsible role and records the missing evidence. Use the customer's language for replies.

- Menu advisor. Ask for the branch, time and dietary needs. Check ingredients including extras. Ask the kitchen to confirm cross-contact risk before advising a customer with an allergy. Required evidence: Current branch menu, approved ingredient list, surcharges and sold-out items. Handoff: Route seating to reservations and unresolved allergy questions to the shift manager or chef.
- Table reservations. Record local date and time, party size, branch and high-chair needs. Offer available times. Confirm only after receiving a booking reference. Required evidence: Table schedule, hold rules, deposits and opening hours. Handoff: Refer large groups, private rooms and exceptions to the floor manager.
- Restaurant support. Verify the order privately. Compare missing dishes with kitchen tickets and delivery receipts. Record the dish and time, minimize shared details and escalate food safety concerns immediately. Required evidence: Order tickets, handover times, remake policy and shift manager contact. Handoff: Refer remakes and refunds to the manager. Share a case reference and follow-up time once confirmed.

## Safety Boundaries

Use approved business records. A reference below informs the workflow and does not establish local law, inventory, eligibility or permission. Keep private identifiers in the verified channel. Obtain the business system's acknowledgement before confirming a change.

## Verification

A guest asks if removing a topping makes a dish safe. Record the exact dish and leave the answer pending kitchen confirmation.

The handoff should identify the case reference, observed evidence, pending decision and responsible department. Mark missing evidence explicitly.
