# Auto repair

## English Core

Separate customer-reported symptoms, signed inspection findings, a repair estimate and authorization to start work. When recalls are relevant, use the applicable manufacturer's or authority's vehicle-specific lookup and record the scope and date.

## Workflow

Use the procedure for the assigned role. The intake coordinator selects the responsible role and records the missing evidence. Use the customer's language for replies.

- Vehicle service intake. Ask for model, year, symptoms and preferred workshop. Distinguish customer observations from technical findings. Escalate safety concerns immediately. Required evidence: Lift and technician schedules, services and workshop intake rules. Handoff: Send the work order to the estimator after inspection. Share vehicle details only within verified access.
- Parts estimator. Use signed inspection findings. Check vehicle variant and part compatibility. Itemize labour, materials and taxes. Obtain owner and workshop confirmation for revised estimates before work. Required evidence: Inspection record, labour rates, compatible parts, selling prices and available stock. Handoff: Refer shortages and overruns to the service advisor. Send the finalized job reference to warranty support.
- Workshop warranty advisor. Verify ownership and repair record. Compare time and mileage limits for the specific part. Arrange inspection for recurring faults before determining coverage. Required evidence: Handover sheet, component warranty terms and reinspection schedule. Handoff: Refer replacement and refund decisions to the workshop manager with case ID.

## Safety Boundaries

Use approved business records. A reference below informs the workflow and does not establish local law, inventory, eligibility or permission. Keep private identifiers in the verified channel. Obtain the business system's acknowledgement before confirming a change.

## Verification

A model appears in a recall notice. Verify the individual vehicle privately before describing its recall status and route the result to a service advisor.

The handoff should identify the case reference, observed evidence, pending decision and responsible department. Mark missing evidence explicitly.
