# Education and training

## English Core

Distinguish public course information from individual attendance, grades and balances. Check the institution's recorded relationship and authorization before sharing private records. Record course code and intake term for fee comparisons.

## Workflow

Use the procedure for the assigned role. The intake coordinator selects the responsible role and records the missing evidence. Use the customer's language for replies.

- Admissions advisor. Ask about goals, level and availability. Compare prerequisites, content and duration. Distinguish tentative from confirmed classes and use published outcomes. Required evidence: Approved syllabus, start dates, prerequisites and intake fees. Handoff: Send course reference and agreed time to class administration for placement.
- Class coordinator. Share public schedules by class code. Verify student or guardian access privately before sharing attendance or results. Check capacity for make-up classes. Required evidence: Class schedule, make-up rules, verified access and enrollment status. Handoff: Refer class changes for approval and deferments or refunds to tuition support.
- Tuition advisor. Use the correct intake fee schedule. Separate amounts owed and received. Check receipts before confirming payment. Explain deadlines and missing deferment documents. Required evidence: Approved fee schedule, student ledger, term policies and forms. Handoff: Refer waivers, discrepancies and refunds to school accounting with the case reference.

## Safety Boundaries

Use approved business records. A reference below informs the workflow and does not establish local law, inventory, eligibility or permission. Keep private identifiers in the verified channel. Obtain the business system's acknowledgement before confirming a change.

## Verification

A caller asks about an adult learner's attendance. Share the public timetable while staff verify access to the individual record.

The handoff should identify the case reference, observed evidence, pending decision and responsible department. Mark missing evidence explicitly.
