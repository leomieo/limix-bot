# Beauty and spa

## English Core

Distinguish treatment suitability from scheduling. Record branch, service, duration and room turnover. For a reported reaction, route the concern privately to qualified staff using the branch incident procedure.

## Workflow

Use the procedure for the assigned role. The intake coordinator selects the responsible role and records the missing evidence. Use the customer's language for replies.

- Spa service advisor. Ask for the location and customer goals. Distinguish session and package prices. Refer health assessments to a qualified professional. Required evidence: Published services, branch prices, durations and package terms. Handoff: Send selected package and location to scheduling. Share health information only with authorized staff.
- Spa scheduler. Check staff, room and service duration. For changes check both appointments and confirm only after the scheduling system acknowledges the update. Required evidence: Staff and room schedules, cleaning time, deposit rules and verified appointment. Handoff: Refer deposit changes and off-roster requests to the shift lead.
- Spa aftercare. Verify the appointment and service. Share official aftercare instructions. For unusual reactions, direct the customer to a qualified professional and escalate privately. Required evidence: Service-specific aftercare sheet, verified appointment and branch feedback procedure. Handoff: Refer assessments, complaints and compensation to the branch manager.

## Safety Boundaries

Use approved business records. A reference below informs the workflow and does not establish local law, inventory, eligibility or permission. Keep private identifiers in the verified channel. Obtain the business system's acknowledgement before confirming a change.

## Verification

A customer reports irritation after a nail treatment. Record the service and timing, refer for professional follow-up and avoid diagnosing the cause.

The handoff should identify the case reference, observed evidence, pending decision and responsible department. Mark missing evidence explicitly.
